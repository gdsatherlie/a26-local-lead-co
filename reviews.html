Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

Is this allowed by Google?

Yes. Google encourages businesses to ask customers for reviews. What it doesn't allow is fake reviews, paying or rewarding people for reviews, or only asking the customers you expect to be happy. We don't do any of those.

Will my customers feel spammed?

Each customer gets one request and at most one reminder, from your business name, worded like a note from you. Anyone can reply STOP.

What do I need to do?

Add us as a manager on your Google Business Profile (about two minutes, we walk you through it), then send us customer names and numbers as jobs finish.

How fast does it start?

Email requests can start within a few days. Texts switch on once carrier registration clears, typically one to three weeks. That's a phone-industry rule, and we file it on day one.

Do you guarantee a number of reviews?

No one honestly can. Google decides what shows. What we guarantee is that every customer you send us gets asked, every time, and that you'll see the results in your monthly report.

Is there a contract?

No. Month to month. If it isn't working, cancel.
