What it does

One receptionist. The whole front desk.

Every call answered in seconds, day or night. No per-minute billing, every feature included.

01

Answers every call, 24/7

Nights, weekends, mid-job. She picks up in seconds, sounds human, never puts anyone on hold, and politely ends robocalls and sales pitches.

02

Trained on your business

Your services, your prices, your hours, your service area. She answers questions like someone who's worked your front desk for years.

03

Books real appointments

Straight onto your calendar, with email confirmations and reminders sent automatically. She only offers open times, so no double-bookings and fewer no-shows.

04

Texts and emails you every lead

The second a call ends: who called, what they need, when they're booked. A text to your phone and a full write-up in your inbox. Nothing waits for you to check voicemail.

05

Missed-call text-back

If a caller hangs up before leaving their details, they get an instant text back with a way to reply or book. Hang-ups become conversations instead of lost jobs. Try it: call (773) 830-3540, hang up after she says hello, and watch your phone.

06

Every call logged

A recording and summary of every call lands in your inbox, and every lead is added to your own Google Sheet. Review any call, any time — you'll know exactly what your receptionist said.

07

Live transfer when it matters

Emergency, or a caller who insists on a person? She takes their name and number first, then connects them to your cell and tells you who's on the line. Optional. Turn it on or off anytime.

08

Never fakes a booking

If a booking doesn't go through, she doesn't pretend it did. She tells the caller you'll call them back, and your alert is marked "call them back" so nothing slips.

Does the receptionist sound like a robot?

What if she can't answer something?

She takes a detailed message and texts you immediately. If you turn on live transfer, urgent callers get connected straight to your cell. Your choice.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. Your number stays yours. We set up simple call forwarding — about two minutes with your carrier, and we walk you through it.

Answering calls within days. Texting features — lead alerts and missed-call text-back — switch on once carrier registration clears, typically one to three weeks. That's a phone-industry rule, not ours, and we file it on day one.

Yes. On its own it's $297 a month plus $497 setup. Added to any plan it's $200 a month. Month to month either way.

A 10-minute form about your business — services, hours, prices, how you want calls handled. We do the rest, and you approve her before she takes a single live call.
