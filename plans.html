Why it works

Most agencies stop at the click. We follow it to the job.

Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your market and how many jobs you can take. We recommend a starting budget in the audit call. You set the cap, and Google bills your card directly.

Who owns the ad accounts?

You do. We work as a manager on your accounts. If you leave, you keep everything: the accounts, the history and the data.

Do you guarantee results?

No honest agency can guarantee a number of leads. We guarantee the work, and we show you cost per lead and cost per booked job every month, so you can judge it yourself.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. Move up or down at the start of any month. Many shops start with Google reviews and add local SEO and ads once they see the results.

Is there a contract?

No. Month to month on every plan. Ads take 60 to 90 days to settle in, so we suggest judging Lead Engine after three months.

Who is this for?

HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing and other home-service shops. Lead Engine fits best for shops with a few trucks that can take on more work.
